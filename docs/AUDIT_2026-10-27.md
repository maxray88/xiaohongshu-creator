## P0 — correctness / silent-failure risks (fix first)

2. `core/humanize_text.py` L96-101: replacement rules eat each other — `因此→所以` then `所以[,，]?` deletes the result; `然而→不过` same. Reorder or mark processed spans.
3. `core/humanize_text.py` L70-71: `\d+[\.、]\s*` eats decimals (`3.14` → `14`); `第[一二…]+` eats `第一次` → `次`. Tighten regexes to require list-marker context.
